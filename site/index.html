03 / Scientific contract

Open methods.
Bounded claims.

Targets stay outside candidate execution. Failed cases stay in the denominator. Worlds receive equal weight. Separate-host replay checks measurements; independent scientific review checks their interpretation.

Governance & conflicts →

Inspect uncertainty

World-level and paired bootstrap intervals, with sample size and limitations visible.

Challenge the metric

Copying a frame can win a consistency measure. Controls and validity studies make that failure visible.

Preserve provenance

Versioned source, data and output hashes. No borrowed benchmark score becomes our own measurement.
