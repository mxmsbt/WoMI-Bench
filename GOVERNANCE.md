# Governance

WoMI-Bench is an independent research project created and maintained by **Maxime Sebti**. It is not presented as a model vendor's benchmark or as a community consortium. Maxime is currently the sole maintainer; additional reviewers and maintainers will be named only after they accept those roles publicly.

## Decisions and conflicts

Protocol proposals, evidence requirements and changes to metric meanings are discussed in public GitHub issues. A metric change requires a version change and preservation of prior result identities. Publishing a new release never silently rewrites a previously measured score.

Any contributor evaluating a model they develop, advise or have a financial interest in must disclose that relationship in its campaign card. This applies to the maintainer and to every entrant. An affiliated model receives the same task contracts, budgets, evidence requirements and admission process as other models. Affiliated submissions require a named unaffiliated reviewer before being labelled independently reviewed. Until that review exists, they remain explicitly self-reported.

No payment buys a rank, data access advantage or an endorsement. Sponsors, if any, will be disclosed here with their role and influence. No external reviewer, sponsor or adopting lab is claimed by this release.

## Reviewable admission

Use the model-submission issue template. Review checks the declared task intersection, frozen model/data/evaluator identities, dataset permissions, attempt policy, complete coverage and raw-evidence replay. A label must distinguish **self-reported**, **replayed by a separate host**, and **reviewed by an unaffiliated person**; they are not interchangeable.

Unresolved scientific objections accompany the result. A credible integrity concern can suspend a result while preserving the original record and reason. Corrections identify both the old and new reports and explain what changed. Appeals are public issues with concrete reproduction steps; sensitive leakage reports may omit active targets.

## Expanding stewardship

The aim is shared technical stewardship with researchers from multiple independent teams. That requires demonstrated contributions and explicit agreement, not a decorative advisory board. Before a model leaderboard becomes a confirmatory ranking, publish its admission checklist, independent reviewers, frozen evaluation campaign and statistical analysis plan.
